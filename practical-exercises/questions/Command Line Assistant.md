## 8. Practical exercise — 15 minutes

Complete this exercise if you have access to a supported RHEL system with Lightspeed configured.

### Exercise checklist

0 of 4 complete

Check your environment

Identify your distribution and version.

cat /etc/os-release

Ask about a command

Identify the command, options, and expected output.

c "Explain the ls -lah command"

Learn about a configuration file

Compare the response with the relevant manual page or official documentation.

c "Explain the purpose of /etc/hosts"

Explore troubleshooting

Identify the diagnostic commands suggested. Do not restart or modify a real service without a reason.

c "How do I check why a systemd service failed?"

If you do not have RHEL Lightspeed installed, practise by writing down the questions you would ask and researching the answers in the `man` pages and official documentation instead.
